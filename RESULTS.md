# Saved LifeLoop strategies

**For your +100% business start, use [Primary’s corrected route](benchmarks/strategies/primary-business.txt).**

## When to act

The date beside an action is the date to tap it. Income arrives on a monthly transition, then you can buy, sell or apply a boost. A boost applies to the **next 120 payments**, starting next month.

For Primary/+100% business:

| Action | Tap on this date | First boosted payment | Last boosted payment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Apply job boost | **12y01m** | 12y02m | 22y01m |
| Buy first Green Fork ($3,006 available) | **19y00m** | — | — |
| Apply business boost | **71y09m** | 71y10m | 81y09m |
| Sell Bright Core with 140% refund; sell everything else for 70% | **89y11m** | — | — |

Advance to **90y00m** only after those sales. The final $140 salary arrives and the game ends immediately. Corrected final cash: **$27,897,093**.

Applying the job boost at 12y02m instead leaves $2,988 at 19y00m, then $3,058 at 19y01m. Apply at 12y01m for the saved route’s 19y00m Green Fork purchase. Ages are years and months, not decimal years.
