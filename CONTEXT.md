# LifeLoop

Education, careers and business investment during one game round.

## Language

**Education level**:
Completed schooling that determines the starting age: None (10), Primary (12),
Secondary (17), College (19), or University (22).
_Avoid_: Calling the education level by its job track's name.

**Job track**:
The salary progression associated with an education level: SunnySproutFields,
FreshMart, BrickLineWorks, ByteFixServices, or DocuDynoCorp, respectively.

**Starting age**:
The age when education finishes. The first monthly payment arrives one month later.

**Starting bonus**:
The income increase selected at the beginning of a round that lasts for the whole
round: +100% job or +50% business in the current comparison.
_Avoid_: Calling it a temporary boost.

**Income boost**:
A once-per-round doubling of job or business income for 120 monthly payments,
multiplying any starting bonus. Job and business boosts have separate uses.

**Refund boost**:
A once-per-round sale of one owned business for 140% of its purchase price.
